Our Story

Built by people who've worked the floor.

LUXE Ambassadors started with a simple idea: Las Vegas deserves a brand ambassador agency run by people who actually understand what makes an event, and a career, work.

Founder Photography
How We Started

Two people, one shared standard for excellence.

Our founders met working high-end activations, galas, and grand openings across the Las Vegas Strip through Elan Artists, one of the industry's respected staffing agencies. Over years of events, they worked alongside — and got to know — some of the most professional, talented, and dependable ambassadors in the city.

That experience taught them two things: clients need talent they can trust without question, and great ambassadors deserve an agency that treats them like the professionals they are. LUXE Ambassadors is the company they wished existed — so they built it.
